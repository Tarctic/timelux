Core Operations & Infrastructure

Three engines behind every shipment

What is torn down does not go to waste. Every tonne we handle moves through three connected capabilities — the yard, the port and the trade lane — so quality is controlled from the first sort to the final bill of lading.

Industrial Processing & Shearing

Heavy machinery and material handlers sort, clean and mechanically shear bulky raw scrap into high-density, furnace-ready dimensions — so it melts faster, yields more and arrives exactly as specified.

Global Logistics & Export Management

End-to-end maritime container freight, customs clearance and cross-border trade compliance through the UAE's major trade corridors — one accountable partner from our yard to your berth.

Established Trade Lanes

Secure, high-volume logistical pipelines delivering premium processed scrap to industrial foundries and mills across India and Asia — built to support repeat, high-volume shipments.

Product Portfolio
